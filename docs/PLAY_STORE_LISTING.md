## App name

RowTool: Row Counter

## Short description

A calm offline row and round counter for knitting and crochet.

## Full description

Count rows. Keep your place. Nothing unnecessary.

RowTool is a calm, tactile row and round counter made for knitting and crochet. Large, clear controls make it easy to keep your place while your hands stay focused on the work.

- Count rows or rounds with large tactile controls.
- Keep several knitting and crochet projects ready to continue.
- Follow your position in a repeating section.
- Set an optional row or round target.
- Correct accidental taps with persistent multi-step undo.
- Edit or reset a count when needed.
- Export a local backup and restore it on another device.

RowTool works completely offline. Your projects stay on your device, and a backup is created only when you choose to export one.

There are no ads, accounts, analytics, subscriptions, or in-app purchases. RowTool is a paid download: pay once and use every included feature.
